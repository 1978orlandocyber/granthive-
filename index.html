The score measures fit between your organization and that grant's stated requirements — not your worthiness. Scores of 80 and above mean strong fit, 60–79 moderate, below 60 weak. If any single check came back "no" on a knockout requirement (like location or nonprofit status), the app flags it for review no matter the score. A low score means "don't spend 40 hours here," not "give up."
