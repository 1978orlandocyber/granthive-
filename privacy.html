Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026
This policy is a starting draft. Have a lawyer review it before you rely on it.

1. The short version

We collect the minimum needed to run the Service: your email (for your account), the organization profile and grants you save, and outcome notes you add. We never sell your data and we never share it with advertisers. AI eligibility checks send your organization profile and the grant description to our AI provider; nothing else leaves your account.

2. What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, stored by our identity provider (Netlify Identity) so you can sign in.
  • Organization profile: the organization details you enter (type, stage, focus area, location, size), stored per-user so eligibility checks and your saved data work across devices.
  • Saved grants and outcomes: grants you save and the applied/won/lost notes you add.
  • Technical data: standard web logs (IP address, browser type) collected by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

We do not collect payment information (the Service is free), and we do not track you across other websites.

3. How we use it

  • To operate your account, run eligibility checks, and sync your saved grants.
  • To improve the Service (for example, understanding which features are used). We do not build advertising profiles.

4. Who processes your data

  • Netlify — hosts the app, manages sign-in (Netlify Identity), and stores your data (Netlify Blobs).
  • Anthropic — our AI provider. When you run an eligibility check, your organization profile and the grant description are sent to Anthropic's API to produce the screening score. We do not send your email or password. Do not paste sensitive personal data (Social Security numbers, bank details, health information) into the app.
  • Public grant sources — federal searches query Grants.gov and award history comes from USAspending.gov. No personal data is sent to these public sources.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone else, except as required by law.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to keep you signed in and to remember app preferences (such as whether you have seen the welcome screen).

6. Your rights and deletion

  • Clear Saved Data in the app deletes your organization profile, saved grants, and outcome notes from your account and your device.
  • To delete your account entirely, contact us (see Section 8) and we will remove your account and its data.
  • You may request a copy of the data we hold about you at any time.

7. Children

GrantComb is intended for adults representing organizations. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the Service evolves; material changes will be noted here with a new "Last updated" date. Questions: contact us through the app's feedback channel (to be published).
